# Product direction

## Purpose

Build a generic Pokémon GO companion that helps players understand current content and prepare for play.

The app is for family use but does not depend on personal Pokémon GO accounts or detailed personal rosters.

## Primary areas

1. Pokémon GO Pokédex
2. Raids
3. Team GO Rocket
4. Eggs
5. Event calendar

A lightweight Today view may summarise current events, raids and alerts.

## Principles

- Prefer useful generic guidance over personal account integration.
- Show assumptions with estimates.
- Use progressive detail: simple lists first, deeper pages where the data supports them.
- Cache external data and retain the last-known-good result.
- Show freshness and source status where current data matters.
- Link related entities across features.

## Out of scope for now

- PokéAPI and generic Pokémon data.
- OpenStreetMap and map plotting.
- Pokémon GO login, account synchronisation or backend access.
- Live spawn, gym, PokéStop or player-location data.
- Detailed family Pokémon roster maintenance.
- Exact Party Play or raid simulation claims.
